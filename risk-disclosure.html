Understand the risk
before you trade.

Trading leveraged financial products involves substantial risk. You should understand the products, costs and potential losses before participating.

Market and leverage risk

Forex, contracts for difference (CFDs), metals, indices and crypto-assets can be volatile. Leverage magnifies both gains and losses. You may lose some or all of the capital allocated to trading, and losses can occur rapidly during volatile markets, gaps or low-liquidity periods.

AI analysis and signals

AI-generated analysis, strategy confluence, confidence estimates and signals are informational decision-support outputs only. A displayed confidence percentage is not a probability guarantee, verified win rate or promise of a profitable result. Model outputs can be incomplete, delayed or incorrect.

Execution and data availability

Chart widgets, market quotes, broker connectivity, automated execution and other integrations are available only where the relevant provider service is configured and operational. A chart display or simulated order is not proof of live broker execution.

No guaranteed returns

BIGBULLS does not promise fixed returns, guaranteed accuracy, zero drawdown or loss-free trading. Past performance and hypothetical examples do not reliably predict future results.

Independent decisions

This website is not personal investment advice. Consider your financial circumstances and obtain independent professional advice where appropriate. Never trade with money you cannot afford to lose.
