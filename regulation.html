INTERNATIONAL JURISDICTIONS

Regional rules.
Entity-specific permissions.

The BIGBULLS group has identified the following markets as relevant to its international regulatory and compliance framework. The regulator, legal entity, licence/registration reference, permitted activities and client coverage must always be read together in the applicable official disclosure.

UNITED KINGDOM

UK regulatory framework

UK financial services permissions and CFD distribution rules are determined by the relevant authorised legal entity and the exact scope of its FCA permissions. A group relationship or company registration alone does not establish authorisation.

EUROPEAN ECONOMIC AREA

European framework

European cross-border services depend on the home-state authorisation, applicable passporting or local permissions, product intervention rules and the client's country of residence. Europe is not one single licence jurisdiction.

AUSTRALIA

Australian framework

Australian financial services activities require the appropriate authorisation or lawful exemption. CFD distribution is subject to ASIC requirements and product-intervention measures where applicable.

CANADA

Canadian framework

Canadian securities and derivatives requirements vary by province and territory. Dealer registration, permitted activities and client eligibility must be checked for the specific legal entity and province.

RUSSIA

Russian framework

Forex-dealer and financial-market activity is subject to Russian law and Bank of Russia oversight. The relevant entity must hold the specific status and permissions required for the service offered.

Important entity-level disclosure: A regulatory licence belongs to the named legal entity, not automatically to every company, brand, affiliate, technology platform or partner in a group. A registration to incorporate a company is not the same as a financial-services authorisation. Client eligibility and product availability can differ between jurisdictions.
WHAT CLIENTS SHOULD VERIFY

Five checks before
opening or funding an account.

01 / LEGAL IDENTITY

Exact contracting entity

Match the complete legal name and registered address shown in the client agreement.

02 / REGULATOR

Official register entry

Use the regulator's own public register, not a logo or third-party directory alone.

03 / LICENCE

Number & current status

Confirm the licence or registration reference is active and belongs to the contracting entity.

04 / PERMISSIONS

Scope of regulated activity

Check that the permissions cover the relevant CFD, forex, dealing, arranging or other service.

05 / CLIENT COVERAGE

Your country & classification

Confirm the entity may lawfully serve your country and client category, and understand any protections that apply.

CFD & LEVERAGED PRODUCTS

Understand the risk
before you trade.

Leverage magnifies outcomes

A relatively small market movement can cause a large gain or loss relative to margin. You may lose some or all of your invested capital.

Costs affect results

Spread, commission, overnight financing, currency conversion, slippage and other charges may apply. Check the relevant entity's current product schedule.

Protections vary

Negative balance protection, leverage limits, close-out rules, client-money treatment and compensation arrangements depend on the applicable entity and jurisdiction.
